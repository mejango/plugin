House rules

  • Maximally accessible Anyone can fund the machine from any chain, in any token. It lives on Ethereum, Base, Optimism, and Arbitrum at once, and whatever a backer pays with gets swapped into the ETH and USDC that back it.
  • Uneditable rules Operations are locked into the machine from deployment. No governance, no admins. What you see is what runs, forever.
  • Issuance doublings Funding the machine issues its tokens. The price to issue doubles at your chosen interval. Early funders get more for less.
  • Cash out anytime Tokens cash out for their share of what the machine holds — or borrow against it to keep an option open. Cashing out pays a 30% tax that stays behind with the holders who stay. All funds used for issuance back the value of all tokens, and only ever leave through cash outs and loans.
* These map to a fixed revnet config. You never touch a curve.
